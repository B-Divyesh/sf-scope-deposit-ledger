Privacy policy · 6 September 2026

See what stays on your device.

This app works without an account, analytics, or a cloud database.

What the app stores

Job details are stored in this browser’s IndexedDB. Theme and license settings use local storage. No job or client record is sent to a server.

Exports and deletion

CSV, PDF, and JSON files are created in your browser. They go only where you choose to save or share them.

Delete a job in the app to remove that record. Clearing this site’s storage removes every local record. Make a JSON backup first.

Sample data

The demo uses a separate IndexedDB database. Resetting or leaving the demo clears its changes without changing your normal ledger.

License verification

If you restore Unlimited, the license token goes to the Sociobot billing API. The app checks it no more than once per day.

Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record. This app never receives your card details.

Tracking and network use

There are no advertising cookies, analytics, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts. The service worker requests this site’s files for offline use.

Privacy requests

No account or cloud ledger exists for us to search or delete. You control local deletion and exports in your browser.

For product privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
